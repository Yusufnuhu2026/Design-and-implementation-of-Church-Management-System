WHO WE ARE

A Church Built on Faith and the Word of God

Welcome to ECWA Church. We are a Christian community committed to worshipping God, sharing the Gospel, building strong relationships and serving humanity.

Our desire is to help individuals and families develop a closer relationship with Jesus Christ through prayer, biblical teaching, fellowship and service.
